How to play

  1. One player holds the screen — that's the describer. Everyone else guesses.
  2. Describe the target word to your team without saying any of the banned words below it.
  3. Your team has to guess the word before time runs out.
  4. Tap "Got it!" when they guess correctly, or "Pass" to skip to the next card.
  5. Score a point for every word your team guesses before the clock hits zero.
