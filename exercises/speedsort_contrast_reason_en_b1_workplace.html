How to play

  1. Each round shows two facts from a workplace case file.
  2. Decide fast: do the facts clash (contrast) or does one cause the other (reason)?
  3. Then pick the connector that fits best.
  4. Try typing the full sentence yourself before you reveal the model answer underneath.
  5. Score points for the right call and the right connector. Ten rounds, then your result.
