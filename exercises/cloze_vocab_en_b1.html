How to play

  1. Tap a word in the word bank, then tap a blank to place it.
  2. Tap a filled blank to send its word back to the bank.
  3. Fill in all 24 blanks across the six worlds of the story.
  4. Press "Check the Story" to see what's correct, wrong, or left blank.
  5. Press "Try Again" to reshuffle the word bank and start over.
