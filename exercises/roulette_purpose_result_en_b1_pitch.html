How to play

  1. Roll the two dice to get a problem and a constraint. Name your invention.
  2. Prepare for 2 minutes. Write keywords, not full sentences.
  3. Pitch for 60 seconds without stopping. Use to, in order to, so that, so … that, such … that.
  4. Answer the panel's questions (about 45 seconds each). Keep talking; fix grammar later.
  5. Tick the structures you used. Roll again and beat your score.
